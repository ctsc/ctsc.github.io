Journey

I was led to the world of Computer Science through my passion for math, software, video games, and curiosity for what drives the world around me. Software and Data is the backbone of everything we do as humans (almost everything we do), and I'm passionate about creating tools that actually help people. 4 months after turning 21, I graduated from Georgia State University with a Bachelors in Computer Science. GSU offered to pay me to complete my masters, so I decided why not stick around one more year to do research. My current research project is on using AI agents and LLMs to optimize quantitative trading strategies for portfolio management. Over the last year I have helped changed the CS culture at GSU by hosting 2x hackathons (largest in GSU history) and helping grow our community of students to 2400 members. There has never been a culture or community like this ever in Georgia, let alone at GSU, and it is super exciting to be a part of the founding team. I am also a competitive powerlifter and President of the Barbell Weightlifting Club.
