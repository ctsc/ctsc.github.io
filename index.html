Hello, World!

Journey

I was led to Computer Science through my passion for math, software, video games, and a curiosity for what drives the world around me. Software and data are the backbone of almost everything we do, and I care about building tools that actually help people.

Four months after turning 21, I graduated from Georgia State University with a Bachelor's in Computer Science. GSU offered to fund my master's, so I stuck around one more year for research. My current project uses AI agents and LLMs to optimize quantitative trading strategies for portfolio management.

Over the last year I have helped change the CS culture at GSU by hosting two hackathons (the largest in GSU history) and growing our student community to 2,400 members. There has never been a culture or community like this in Georgia, let alone at GSU, and it is exciting to be part of the founding team. I am also a competitive powerlifter and President of the Barbell Weightlifting Club.

This section stays personal on purpose. For internships and the professional side, see my LinkedIn.
